## 3. Programs & projects

### Current (2025–26, from team notes — NOT on the live site)
1. **Afar Region — Digital Classrooms:** fully equipped computers pre-loaded with offline educational materials donated to schools.
2. **Debre Birhan University — Dignity Kits:** sanitary pad (Modess) support for female students who cannot afford them.
3. **Shewa Robit — School Materials:** supplies for students without family/guardian support.
4. **Youth Sports Development, Debre Birhan:** sporting materials for 85+ youths with government + Tesfa Sports Center (coaches); 2 trainees advanced to the Addis Ababa Academy.
5. **Annual Sports Festival:** athletics + football competition with prizes.
6. **Single Mothers Tailoring Project (completed):** trainer hired, machines + materials purchased, government-facilitated workspace; machines handed over — women now independently manufacture clothing/undergarments.
7. **Addis Ababa Health Insurance:** digital health insurance covering annual medical expenses for 50+ disabled and elderly people.
8. **Gamo Landslide Relief:** ~500,000 ETB donated to disaster victims.

### Legacy (from the live site, 2022–23)
- IDP support Debre Berhan (food, hygiene, shelter; 2022, 2023) · Financial support Shewa Robit (2022) · Ataye technical/material support incl. hospital generator restoration (2022)
- Livelihood Shewa Robit: 45 women received 3,000–5,000 ETB soft loans; 26 young women recruited from bars/restaurants for income-generation training
- Cooperative Debre Berhan: 20 women trained in sewing (with Muluwengel Church)
- 30 computer sets to Shewa-Robit High School (2022, with Camara Education); 30 students accepted for support
- Planned: Geferssa Water Rehabilitation Center (27 ha, 22 km from Addis, 200 m+ well) · Wag Himra livelihood project

### Strategic pillars (from Preamble/Who We Are)
1. Emergency response (disasters, IDPs, food & sanitation)
2. Vulnerability & poverty (violence, domestic abuse, FGM, forced marriage, single mothers)
3. Children with special needs (autism, disability, mental well-being, stigma reduction)

## 5. UX / technical audit of current site

**Critical**
- Contact Us in main nav links to a **staging domain** (nkd.ker.mybluehost.me) — dead trust signal.
- **No donation flow at all** — the single most important NGO conversion is a bank account number in body text.
- Volunteer "registration" page has **no form** (only cookie-consent UI renders).
- Newest content is 2022–23; the org's most compelling work (sports academy, tailoring co-op, health insurance, Gamo relief) is absent.

**Major**
- Three different mission/vision statements across Home, Who We Are, About Us, Preamble.
- Typos throughout: "REGEISTER", "finical compensation", "news later", "Discovery Our Programs".
- Duplicate pages (About Us vs Who We Are vs Preamble) with overlapping content.
- Generic Elementor template: clipart birds/clouds, accordion walls of text, no visual hierarchy.
- Social links say "Coming soon" (since 2022).

**Content strengths to amplify**
- The motto YAGEBANAL — first-person moral claim, trilingual potential (Amharic/English/Swedish).
- Real, specific numbers: 85+ youths, 50+ insured elders, 500K ETB, 130 children/month, 45 loans, 30 computers, 20 women trained.
- Diaspora bridge story: Umeå ↔ Addis Ababa.
- Credible, senior leadership bios.
- The tailoring co-op arc ("they run it themselves now") = perfect flagship story of dignity, not charity.
